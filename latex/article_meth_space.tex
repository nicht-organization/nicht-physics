\section{Introduction \& Subject-Space Duality}
\label{sec:intro}
Classical mathematical epistemology projects constructive $1$-logic uniformly across both the observer and space ($1 \to 1 \to 1$), treating formal verification as an intrinsic property of reality \cite{russell1903}. Under \textit{Nicht-Theorie}, we suspend this projection to examine the open ontological relations between consistency modes ($0$-consistency vs.\ $1$-consistency) and temporal dynamics \cite{nicht_theory_monograph, nicht_physics_paper_1}.

\subsection{Ontological Spheres: Atemporal Baselines vs.\ Dynamic $1$-Apertures}
To maintain structural compatibility across physical and logical domains without prejudging the nature of mathematical space, we map the broader domain landscape \cite{nicht_theory_monograph, nicht_physics_paper_1}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{$0$-Consistent Baselines ($B_0$):} Abstract ideas, the unformed potential of the future, and the unalterable sediment of elapsed history (the past) are inherently atemporal \cite{nicht_theory_monograph, nicht_physics_paper_1}. Operating under zero assertion pressure, they require no state enforcement and carry zero operational latency ($\mathcal{W} = 0$) \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}.
    \item \textbf{$1$-Consistent Paradoxical Spheres:} Material space and the active present (\textit{Reality Now}) function as transient, highly volatile apertures \cite{nicht_physics_paper_1}. Here, time manifests as dynamic movement---a continuous, restless negation of $0$ ("ever and never") that generates observational noise ($\sigma$) and structural operational load ($\mathcal{W}$) \cite{nicht_theory_monograph, nicht_physics_paper_1}. Perspectively and apophatically woven into the active present ("here \& now"), neither consistency nor inconsistency is deniable: consistency is demanded by the evaluating aperture, while inconsistency inevitably manifests through temporal and spatial limits.
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Space (Open Hypothesis):} We leave the ontological mode of mathematical space explicitly open in this introductory phase, posing it as a formal candidate whose consistency mode ($0$ vs.\ $1$) will be tested via \textit{reductio ad absurdum} in Section~\ref{sec:reductio}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Mathematical Subject: Lifetime Conflict and Open Options}
Epistemologically, human and formal observers perceive the "mathematical subject" primarily through its structural trace as a bounded \textit{mathematical object}. This introduces an existential "lifetime conflict"---a structural paradox between acting as an autonomous evaluating subject/space or remaining a mere object bound by formal limits \cite{godel1931, nicht_theory_monograph}. We leave the resolution of this conflict open to the reader across two primary configurations:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Option A (The Subject as a Bounded $1$-Object/Aperture):} The subject operates within temporal dynamics ($t \in \mathbb{R}^+$), where time continually negates the static $0$-baseline. Bound as a mere object, it wrestles with active assertion pressure ($P_A$) and passive substrate tension ($P_{\text{pass}}$), perpetually caught in Gödelian unprovability and operational latency \cite{poincare1908, godel1931}.
    \item \textbf{Option B (The Subject as an Atemporal $0$-Space):} If the subject overcomes its object-bound aperture constraints, its assertion pressure collapses into complete apophatic relaxation \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}. In this limit, the subject becomes co-extensive with atemporal mathematical space itself ($B_0$), achieving latency-free invariance free from temporal decay \cite{hilbert1912, nicht_theory_monograph}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Coupled Pressure Dynamics: Necessity, Sufficiency, and Passive Tension}
Systemic pressure within any evaluation system is not merely a binary switch between active and zero states \cite{nicht_physics_paper_1}. It is driven by the interaction of \textit{Necessity Pressure} ($P_N$, the ontological requirement to exist) and \textit{Sufficiency Pressure} ($P_S$, the constructive requirement of assertion completeness), balanced against \textit{Active Pressure} ($P_A$) and \textit{Passive Substrate Tension} ($P_{\text{pass}}$) \cite{nicht_physics_paper_1, nicht_physics_special_1}:
\begin{equation}
P_{\text{total}}(t) = P_A(t) + P_{\text{pass}}(t) + \lambda \cdot \Big( P_N(t) \circ P_S(t) \Big)
\end{equation}
where $\circ$ denotes the coupled \textit{Necessary \& Sufficient} relation of formal state assertion \cite{nicht_physics_special_1}. Within this coupled operator relation, sufficiency is sufficient (enough) and (at least) necessary for necessity, and vice versa ($P_S \iff P_N$) inside the evaluating aperture. When temporal movement (the dynamic negation of $0$) ceases or relaxes via the apophatic collapse operators ($\mathcal{D}_A, \mathcal{D}_S$), the necessary and sufficient pressures uncouple, driving total system tension back to the invariant zero-baseline ($B_0$) \cite{nicht_physics_paper_1, nicht_physics_special_1}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{D}_S\left[ P_S(t) \right] \implies \lim_{t \to \infty} \Big( P_A(t) + P_{\text{pass}}(t) + P_N(t) \circ P_S(t) \Big) = 0 \implies B_0 \quad \text{($0$-Consistency)}
\end{equation}

\section{\texorpdfstring{Reductio ad Absurdum of Active Spatial $1$-Consistency}{Reductio ad Absurdum of Active Spatial 1-Consistency}}
\label{sec:reductio}

To establish the operator-theoretic necessity of an unasserted baseline ($B_0$), we evaluate the formal logical consequences of projecting active $1$-logic directly onto mathematical space \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}.

\subsection{Formal Hypothesis}
\textbf{Hypothesis ($H_1$):} Assume that continuous mathematical space is inherently $1$-consistent, requiring active, continuous state verification ($P_A > 0$) and structural sufficiency ($P_S > 0$) across every spatial coordinate to sustain valid topological existence \cite{nicht_physics_paper_1, nicht_physics_paper_3}.

\subsection{Deductive Derivation of Systemic Paradoxes}

\subsubsection{1. Measure-Theoretic \& Operational Latency Divergence}
If every point in a continuous spatial manifold $M$ demands active positive assertion ($P_A > 0$), the operational density over an uncountably infinite measure $\mu(M) > 0$ accumulates infinite computational latency ($\mathcal{W}$) and measurement noise ($\sigma$) \cite{nicht_physics_paper_1, nicht_physics_special_1}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{W}(M) = \int_M \left( P_A(x) + P_S(x) \right) d\mu(x)
\end{equation}
Because $\mu(M)$ is continuous and $P_A(x) > 0$ everywhere under $H_1$, the integral diverges identically \cite{nicht_physics_paper_3}:
\begin{equation}
\lim_{P_S \to \text{full}} \mathcal{W}(M) = \infty \quad \implies \quad \sigma(M) \to \infty
\end{equation}
Under active $1$-consistency, the space instantaneously collapses under its own unbounded operational saturation ("analytic noise-collapse") \cite{nicht_theory_monograph, nicht_physics_paper_3}.

\subsubsection{2. Gödelian Self-Fracturation}
Enforcing self-referential sufficiency ($P_S > 0$) across continuous spatial elements requires the space to continuously evaluate its own formal consistency from within its own axiomatic aperture \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}. By Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems and Tarski's undefinability theorem, this forces self-referential truth predicates into undecidable infinite oscillations ($G \leftrightarrow \neg \text{Proof}(G)$) \cite{godel1931, nicht_theory_monograph}:
\begin{equation}
\forall x \in M, \quad \text{Eval}(x) \implies \text{Recursive Meta-Hierarchy Fracture}
\end{equation}
Spatial continuity breaks down into an uncountably infinite cascade of meta-axiomatic sub-apertures \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}.

\subsubsection{3. Zenoic Trajectory Freeze}
In classical mechanics and differential geometry, dynamic trajectories are modeled over smooth continuous parameters $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Under $H_1$, moving through space requires exhaustively asserting each discrete sub-interval state ($\sum D/2^n$) under active $1$-logic \cite{nicht_theory_monograph, aristotle_physics}. The accumulated partition latency per unit length diverges:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{W}_{\text{motion}} = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \mathcal{W}\left( \frac{D}{2^n} \right) \to \infty
\end{equation}
This infinite operational latency locks all continuous state transitions into an absolute, static freeze \cite{nicht_theory_monograph, nicht_physics_paper_4}.

\subsection{Q.E.D. Conclusion}
Because the premise $H_1$ leads to measure-theoretic noise divergence, Gödelian structural fracturation, and Zenoic dynamic freeze, $H_1$ is strictly false \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}. 

Mathematical space cannot be an active, positively asserted $1$-consistent entity. Therefore, spatial continuity is necessarily $0$-consistent ($B_0$), existing as an unasserted, invariant baseline \cite{nicht_theory_monograph, nicht_physics_paper_1}:
\begin{equation}
\text{Space} \neq 1\text{-Consistency} \quad \implies \quad \text{Space} \equiv 0\text{-Consistency } (B_0)
\end{equation}

\section{Hilbert-Space Langevin Dynamics \& Apophatic Relaxation}
\label{sec:langevin}

\subsection{State Vector Dissipation in Hilbert Space}
Let $\psi(t) \in \mathcal{H}$ denote the state vector of an active evaluating subject/aperture \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}. The departure from the unasserted baseline $B_0$ is driven by the total system pressure $P_{\text{total}}(t) = P_A(t) + P_{\text{pass}}(t) + \lambda (P_N(t) \circ P_S(t))$ \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}. The temporal evolution of $\psi(t)$ obeys the stochastic Hilbert-Langevin equation \cite{langevin1908, nicht_theory_monograph}:
\begin{equation}
\frac{d}{dt} |\psi(t)\rangle = -\frac{1}{\hbar} \hat{\mathcal{H}}_0 |\psi(t)\rangle - \gamma_A \mathcal{D}_A \left[ P_A(t) \right] |\psi(t)\rangle + \xi(t)
\end{equation}
where $\hat{\mathcal{H}}_0$ is the unperturbed baseline operator, $\gamma_A > 0$ is the apophatic damping coefficient, $\mathcal{D}_A$ is the assertion decay operator, and $\xi(t) \in \mathcal{H}$ represents stochastic aperture fluctuations satisfying \cite{langevin1908, nicht_theory_monograph}:
\begin{equation}
\langle \xi(t) \rangle = 0, \quad \langle \xi(t) \xi^\dagger(t') \rangle = 2 \gamma_A \cdot \text{Noise}_{\text{aperture}} \, \delta(t - t') \hat{I}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Apophatic Relaxation Operators}
The operator $\mathcal{D}_A$ governs the exponential dissipation of active assertion pressure $P_A(t)$ under environmental aperture noise $\sigma(t)$ \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}:
\begin{equation}

Simultaneously, the apophatic collapse operator $\mathcal{D}_S$ acts upon the coupled Necessity/Sufficiency pressure $P_N(t) \circ P_S(t)$ \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}:

\subsection{Asymptotic Convergence to $0$-Consistency}
In the infinite temporal horizon ($t \to \infty$), active state assertion and passive substrate tension completely dissipate \cite{nicht_theory_monograph}:
\begin{equation}
\lim_{t \to \infty} P_{\text{total}}(t) = \lim_{t \to \infty} \left( P_A(t) + P_{\text{pass}}(t) + \lambda \cdot [P_N(t) \circ P_S(t)] \right) = 0 \implies B_0
